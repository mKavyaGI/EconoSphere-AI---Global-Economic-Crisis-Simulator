# Phase 16 Step 3: Leakage and Timing Audit

## CPI / Inflation (HIGH_PRIORITY_FEASIBLE)
- **Forecast Timing Assumption**: End of Year (Dec 31)
- **Frequency**: Monthly
- **Aggregation**: YOY trailing or latest available month
- **Publication Lag**: ~15 to 45 days
- **Max Permitted Source Date**: Nov 30 (due to December lag crossing the Dec 31 boundary)
- **Leakage Risk**: LOW if source date strict cutoff is enforced.
- **Eligibility**: Eligible for future experiments.
