# Project Requirements

## Mission
Simulate the world's economy, predict the impact of global events, and help governments, businesses, and researchers evaluate policy decisions.

## Core Features
- **Global Economic Digital Twin:** Entity modeling of countries and currencies.
- **Trade Network Graph:** Visualizing deep interdependencies.
- **Crisis Scenario Simulation:** (e.g. Pandemics, War, Oil Shocks, Tariffs).
- **AI Forecasting:** Predict impact on GDP, Inflation, Unemployment.
- **Policy Engine:** Recommend AI-driven mitigations.
